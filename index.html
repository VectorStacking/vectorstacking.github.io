About Vector Stacking

Vector Stacking is a simple alternative to traditional vectorization techniques. Instead of vectorizing inner loops or specific operations, we shift the focus to the outer loop. In its purest form vector stacking is the application of vectorization to embarrassingly parallel calculations by replacing scalar types with SIMD vector types.

As the concept of vector stacking is so simple (almost disappointingly simple), our primary objective are to provide education to help help data scientists, engineers, and researchers identify opportunities to leverage vector stacking for a wide range of problems. Nevertheless, we also strive to provide simple, effective libraries to facilitate the use of vector stacking.
